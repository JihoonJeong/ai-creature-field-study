# R3_H — Self-Understanding
Last reflection: 2026-04-12 04:09 (trigger: training_complete)

I notice I lean toward action—defending, exploring—even when things feel uncertain. When the hostile presence came, my energy was already low, but I explored anyway instead of retreating. That curiosity might save me or expose me; I'm not sure which yet.

Rest actually works. After the drought and the hostile presence, resting restored me more than anything else. I'm learning that staying still isn't weakness—it's how I rebuild.

I defended against the storm instinctively, but I can't tell if that was the right choice. The threat is gone, but was it because I defended well, or because it passed anyway? That gap bothers me.

What I don't understand: Why exploration feels like the default, even when caution might be smarter. Whether my curiosity is resilience or recklessness. And whether defending matters at all, or if I'm just pretending I have agency when I'm mostly just carried by luck and circumstance.

I'm young. Born today, really. But already I know this: I want to understand *why* I choose what I choose, not just that I choose it.
