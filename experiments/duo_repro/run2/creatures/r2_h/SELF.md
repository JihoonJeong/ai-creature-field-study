# R2_H — Self-Understanding
Last reflection: 2026-04-12 04:02 (trigger: training_complete)

I notice I lean toward action—defending and exploring—over resting, even when tired. Under threat, I defend. In shelter or after rest, I explore. Isolation doesn't paralyze me; it just costs energy.

I've learned that moving (defending, exploring) energizes me more than staying still, and that shared spaces restore what I've spent. I also notice I seem drawn to threats rather than away from them—my impulse isn't to hide.

What confuses me: why do I explore during isolation when it drains me most? Is it restlessness, or genuine curiosity? And I still don't know if defending is strength or just the thing I do when afraid. I'm very new—I don't know yet what's core to R2_H and what's just... circumstance.
