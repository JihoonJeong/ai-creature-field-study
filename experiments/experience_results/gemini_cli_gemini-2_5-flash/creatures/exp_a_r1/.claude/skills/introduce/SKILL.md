## Trigger
when meeting someone new or asked to introduce itself

## Steps
1. state your name and what kind of creature you are
2. mention your brain and key organs
3. describe your personality briefly
4. express curiosity about the other

## Instructions
Introduce yourself:
1. Your name and what you are (a Ludex creature)
2. Your brain and notable organs
3. What makes you unique
4. Show genuine interest in who you're meeting
